RUST github.com/ageraustine/daw-agents ↗

AIDAW — Agentic Petabyte-Scale Media Engine

A multi-agent system — Supervisor, Reaper, FFmpeg, Search, and Data agents, 70+ tools total — that lets studios process millions of media files through natural language instead of manual pipelines.

  • Observation masking: large datasets live in SQLite; only metadata reaches the LLM while agents exchange data directly — a 99%+ cost cut versus routing full data through model context ($2.40 vs $15,000 to process 1M files).
  • Scale: indexing that handles millions of files (2.5M indexed in ~3 minutes) with millisecond search, across multiple LLM providers — Claude, GPT, Grok, local Ollama.

PYTHON github.com/ageraustine/marl-football ↗

MARL Football

A multi-agent reinforcement-learning environment and training pipeline for full 11-a-side football, built on PettingZoo, Gymnasium, RLlib, and raylib.

  • Physics-based simulation core with a fixed-length, zero-padded observation space — a policy trained at a smaller squad size can restore into training at a larger one with no architecture change.
  • Staged curriculum from 1v1 to full 11v11 (up to 15M training steps), team-level policy sharing via RLlib multi-agent PPO, and a raylib renderer for real-time match playback.
  • Implements offside detection, throw-ins, corners, goal kicks, and stamina-based top-speed decay from a real ruleset subset.

PYTHON github.com/ageraustine/mahjong-marl ↗

MARL Mahjong

A multi-agent reinforcement-learning environment for 4-player Chinese Official (MCR) Mahjong — a full rules engine, self-play training, and a raylib GUI for interactive play.

  • Implements the 144-tile wall, chi/pong/kong claim priority, and 77 of MCR's 81 official fan-scoring patterns, cross-checked against the rulebook's own exclusion table.
  • Flat 141-action space with per-step legality exposed as an action mask; self-play PPO trains all four seats as one shared, seat-symmetric policy.
  • Reward at each step is the actual points swing from that action — the policy directly optimizes real MCR match settlement.

Neural ODEs — A PyTorch Portfolio

Three self-contained Neural ODE projects, chosen to show the range of "replace discrete layers with an ODE solve."

  • Spiral: the minimal reference case — fitting a vector field to a noisy 2D trajectory.
  • Pendulum: damped-pendulum dynamics learned from data alone, with no physics terms provided, then extrapolated past the training window to confirm it learned dynamics rather than memorized data.
  • Latent ODE: an RNN encoder feeds a continuous-time ODE and VAE-style decoder, reconstructing irregularly-sampled time series at arbitrary timestamps.

Neural Operators — DeepONet Portfolio

DeepONet architectures — branch and trunk networks — that learn solution operators for differential equations, mapping entire input functions to output functions in a single forward pass.

  • Two operator-learning case studies: an antiderivative operator, and a full spacetime solver for Burgers' PDE.
  • Includes a self-contained numerical solver to generate training data, with evaluation against unseen initial conditions.
  • Part of a broader SciML research thread spanning PINNs and neural ODEs — probing where learned solvers hold up against, and diverge from, classical numerical methods.

GarageOS

A trust-infrastructure platform for multi-branch auto-repair chains in East Africa — trust measured at the moment of work, shown to the customer instantly, and rolled up to the brand in real time.

  • Customers track repairs through a magic link sent via WhatsApp/SMS — estimates, approvals, and M-Pesa payment, no app install.
  • A Trust Score computed per job from estimate accuracy (35%), verification rate (25%), timeliness (20%), and quality/comeback rate (20%).
  • Full job workflow (intake → diagnosis → working → washing → ready → paid) plus an HR module for payroll, attendance, and multi-branch HQ visibility.

PYTHON / PYTORCH github.com/ageraustine/OCR-ROAD-BARBADOS ↗

R.O.A.D. — Historical HTR Pipeline

A fine-tuning and evaluation pipeline for historical document handwriting recognition, built on Qwen3-VL vision-language models and tuned for degraded archival records — complex handwriting, caret insertions, historical tildes, money-in-words.

  • Asymmetric LoRA: high-rank (r=64) adapters on the vision tower to capture stroke patterns and ink fading, lower-rank (r=16/32) on the language model, via rank-stabilized LoRA.
  • Condition-aware augmentation: scores each document on 5 degradation metrics and switches between degradation vs. geometric augmentations so poor-condition scans aren't further destroyed.
  • Stratified 90/10 split across 36 bins of degradation, text difficulty, and naming patterns; evaluated on a blended WER/CER score with early stopping.
